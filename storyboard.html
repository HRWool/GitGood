Final feature list

🚀 Onboarding

  • Sign up with a UNCC email only
  • Username + avatar
  • Pick skills + flag custom skills

🧠 Adaptive learning

  • Short, skippable first quest
  • Microlessons framed as customer requests
  • Timed coding practice
  • Pathway that unlocks in order

🔥 Engagement

  • Streaks, streak freezes, at-risk reminders
  • 3 daily quests + bonus chest
  • XP, levels, mystery-chest rewards
  • Notification preferences

🏆 Leaderboard

  • App / Class / Friends scopes
  • Centered on your row with exact XP gap
  • Suggested quest sized to close the gap

🎓 AI Classrooms

  • 4–6 seasonal skill tracks
  • Prof. Byte posts weekly topics + quests
  • Class leaderboards reset each season

🏅 Achievements

  • Trophy case with locked “to-do” badges
  • Share link, LinkedIn, resume line, QR
  • Public verify page — no login needed

Out of scope for now: AI project-idea generator, AI resume feedback, employer accounts (replaced by linkable, verifiable achievements).
